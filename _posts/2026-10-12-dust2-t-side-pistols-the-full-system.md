Pistol rounds on Dust2 are decided by the opening script and the first decision after contact. The teams that win them start with mid control, assign one clear contact role, and only branch once they have information. Here is the full system with the execution details, timings, and variations used by Spirit, Vitality, Falcons, and NAVI, plus a 2026 breakdown of how every top IGL (apEX, magixx, karrigan, Aleksib, FalleN and Twistzz) builds the round.

## Why This Round Matters So Much

The T pistol is an underdog round on Dust2. Across HLTV's full Dust2 history, Ts win only 47.3% of pistols, but the T team that wins it converts round 2 80.8% of the time ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/stats/maps/map/31/Dust2)). Win it and you are usually 3–0 before the CTs touch a rifle. Lose it and you spend two rounds into SMG anti-ecos on the longest sightlines in the pool. That swing is why every elite IGL treats it as a prepared set piece, not a default.

Dust2 survived every pool change this year. Anubis came back in January and Cache replaced Overpass on July 8, 2026, so the pool is now Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust2, Inferno, Mirage and Nuke ([dust2.us](https://www.dust2.us/news/75298/breaking-valve-announce-the-return-of-cache-to-active-duty-after-seven-years), [Gamertag Mythras](https://gamertagmythras.com/blog/counter-strike-2/cs2-active-duty-map-pool-history)). The same Season 5 update reworked bomb damage so the blast weakens around corners and stops at walls ([SkinSniper](https://skinsniper.com/events/cs2-season-5)). That changes pistol post-plants; more on it below.

## Buys and Roles

Most players run Glock. One player buys a P250 and armor. That player is the contact player and takes the first mid-range fight. One kit is optional on the lurk or plant player. Utility is one Xbox or mid smoke and one flash. Armor is non-negotiable on the contact player. The P250 goes to the player most likely to win the first duel, not the player who asks for it.

**Contact player (donk style)**  
P250 + armor. Plays short or mid. Creates the opening. If this player gets the kill, flood. Does not lurk.

**Second short player (NiKo style)**  
Trades the first contact or takes the second angle. Can throw the supporting flash or smoke. Supports the entry rather than pure lurking.

**Support / late angle (m0NESY style)**  
Avoids the first dry fight. Holds a longer angle once utility is down or throws a flash after space is taken. Secondary contact or support on pistol.

**IGL / mid connector (karrigan or Aleksib style)**  
Calls the Xbox smoke. Coordinates utility. Reads the first 10–15 seconds and decides the branch. Stays with mid or short rather than forcing early contact. Prefers space before full commitment. Can play the mid-to-lower tunnels link.

**Fifth player**  
Pure lurk or tunnels watcher. Stays quiet until the main fight creates a sound.

### The Buy Menu

Every T starts with $800, so the round is a question of how to split it across five players. The Glock's strength is close-range spray and running accuracy, which is why Dust2's short, cornered entries (upper tunnels into B, catwalk onto A) suit it far more than the open long doors.

| Buy | Cost | Left over | When pros use it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kevlar | $650 | $150 | Mid fights and long duels against USP headshots |
| P250 + flash | $500 | $300 | One-tap power on short and through B doors; flash for the entry |
| Tec-9 | $500 | $300 | Running spam on B rushes; rarer since CT kevlar blunts it |
| Smoke + flash | $500 | $300 | Utility carrier on a hit (Xbox, CT or window smoke + entry flash) |
| 2× flash + molotov | $800 | $0 | Lead entry on B; molotov for back plat or car |
| Dual Berettas | $300 | $500 | Rare; close-range B with leftover for a flash or two |

The usual template is two or three utility buyers, the P250 + armor contact player, and the lurker on a cheap flash or plain Glock. Pros almost never buy five kevlars on the Dust2 T pistol, because the hit dies to utility first and to aim second.

### The CT Setups You Are Hitting Into

- **2–1–2 standard:** two A (long + short/site), one mid (doors or CT mid), two B. The most common setup, and the one most T pistols are designed to overload.
- **Stacked B (3 B):** a third player rotating early through CT mid to B, often with kevlar and an HE for tunnels. This punishes the five-man tunnel rush.
- **Aggressive long:** a CT pushing long corner or pit with a flash to trade a pick early and make the A side burn time.
- **Mid-doors info:** a CT peeks or smokes mid early to see where Ts commit, then calls rotations before the bomb is down.

A good T pistol answers two questions in its first 20 seconds: where is the extra CT, and can we get the bomb down before the rotation lands.

## Default Opening (0–15 seconds)

One player throws the Xbox smoke immediately. Two players move to short as soon as it lands. One of them is the P250 contact player. One player holds outside long and does not peek. One player waits mid or lurks long. The fifth stays available.

The smoke denies the CT mid player a free kill. Short players do not need kills. They need to exist so the team can choose. This is the shared starting point across the teams above.

Spirit's Budapest Major version against Falcons added a short molotov after the Xbox smoke. Three players climbed cat. A flash was thrown once the molotov ended. Chopper smoked CT. Short players swung through the defensive smoke. Donk took two with the P250. Two players timed long. They took A and won a 5v3 post-plant. Vitality later countered the same structure by placing a player under the drop who killed the first player coming down.

Vitality's variation leans harder on mid presence. The mid duo creates contact or forces utility. Lurkers on long and short stay quiet until CTs commit, then take the empty side or the late plant. Newer stacks can copy the principle: two mid (one with P250), one lurk long, one short, one waiting. The mid players only need to create a sound or a death that tells the team which site is lighter.

## Decision Rules (15–25 seconds)

After the smoke lands and short is taken:

- Short free or only light contact → call A hit.
- Heavy long early or multiple CTs long → call B rush.
- P250 player gets the first kill → flood that site.
- First two players die without a trade → remaining three do not re-peek the same angle. Rotate or save.

These are the only branches. Everything else is freestyle and loses rounds.

## A Hit Execute

Two players long, two players short or cat, one lurk. Throw a long or CT smoke if available. Long players take the first contact. Cat players wait for the sound and swing. Do not both peek dry at the same time. Plant default or for long. Post-plant holds: one long, one site, one CT or mid. Do not chase. The lurk only moves once the main fight has started.

Timing note: utility should land with roughly 1:20–1:30 remaining so the team has time to clear and plant without being forced into a late retake.

## B Rush

All five players tunnels. One flash if available. Do not stop in tunnels. Plant default. Hold window and site. If the first two die without a trade, the rest save or full rotate. Speed is the only advantage. FURIA and Spirit have both used versions of this when they want a fast plant and a simple post-plant.

The B rush is the pistol most exposed to a stacked B. If the opponent has shown a third B player or a tunnels HE in earlier maps, run the mid-to-B split instead: three upper tunnels, two through mid doors to lower tunnels or CT mid, cutting the rotator.

## Additional Variations

**Mid fake into B**  
Two players make noise or throw utility mid or short. Three players are already in tunnels. The fake lasts 8–12 seconds. If CTs rotate, commit B. If they do not, continue the A hit instead of forcing B.

**Long timing lurk**  
One player goes long alone and waits. The other four play mid or short. The lurk does not peek first. He moves only after hearing the main fight. NAVI and Spirit have used this to punish early over-rotates. For newer players the rule is simple: if you are the lurk, you wait for the sound.

**Distraction peek**  
One player wide peeks or bunny hops long or mid with no intention of winning the duel. The only job is to force a CT to shoot and reveal. The other four are already moving to the opposite site. Only run this if the team knows the plan. Otherwise the distraction player dies for free.

**A split (long + short)**  
Two long, two short, one mid lurk. The classic Dust2 crossfire hit. Strong against a 2–1–2 because the A CTs have to choose one angle.

## Post-Plant Defaults

- **A site**: one long, one site, one CT or mid. Do not stack site.
- **B site**: one window, one site, one mid or CT. Window is the priority hold.
- One player always watches the most likely CT entry (CT for A, mid for B).

Since the July bomb-damage rework, a CT who saves behind a wall survives the explosion ([SkinSniper](https://skinsniper.com/events/cs2-season-5)). On pistol that matters twice: a lost retake no longer guarantees the CT dies, and a saved pistol or kevlar changes the round 2 anti-eco. If the round 2 economy matters, hunt the saver instead of sitting on the bomb.

## Utility Timing Details

Utility on pistol is limited (usually one smoke and one flash). Timing is what makes it effective. The goal is to land the smoke before players expose, use the flash to cover the swing, and avoid throwing utility that lands after the fight has already started. Here are the timings that match the structures used by Spirit, Vitality, and similar teams.

### Xbox / Mid Smoke Timing

Throw the Xbox smoke immediately after freeze time. It should land and bloom before the short players reach the corner of short. This takes roughly 2–3 seconds from throw to full cover. Short players start moving as soon as the throw is called, not after the smoke has already landed. If they wait for the smoke to fully bloom before moving, they lose the timing window and give CTs time to push short or mid.

In Spirit's Budapest Major version against Falcons, Chopper threw the Xbox smoke first, then jumped down and molotov'd short. The molotov was used to delay or clear short while the rest of the team climbed cat. The flash came after the molotov ended so players could swing through the defensive smoke CTs threw. The sequence was smoke → molotov → flash → swing. Newer teams can drop the molotov and keep only the smoke + flash if money is tight.

### Flash Timing

The flash is thrown once short or long players are in position to swing, not earlier. On short, the flash should pop as the contact player reaches the edge of short or just before the swing. A flash thrown too early lets CTs turn away. A flash thrown too late leaves players peeking dry.

On an A hit, the long flash is thrown as the long players reach the doors or just before they peek. The cat or short flash is thrown on the sound of the long contact so the second angle arrives while CTs are already looking the other way. Do not throw both flashes at the same time with no coordination. One flash covers the first contact. The second covers the trade swing.

### Long or CT Smoke Timing

If a second smoke is available, throw the long or CT smoke after the Xbox smoke has already given short control. The long smoke should land before the long players commit past the doors. The CT smoke is used once the team has decided to hit A and wants to block the rotate. Throwing the CT smoke too early wastes it if the team ends up going B. Throwing it too late leaves the rotate open during the plant.

### B Rush Utility

On a full B rush there is usually no smoke. If one flash is available, throw it as the first players reach the end of tunnels, just before they exit onto site. The flash should pop as the entry players clear the doors or window angle. Do not throw the flash from deep in tunnels. It will pop behind the entry players and do nothing.

### Decision Timing Relative to Utility

The Xbox smoke creates a 15–20 second window of information. The IGL should make the site call inside that window, not after the smoke has fully faded. If short is free while the smoke is still up, call the A hit or mid-to-B before the smoke dies. If heavy long contact happens while the smoke is still active, call B immediately. Waiting until the smoke fades to decide usually means the team is already late and CTs have rotated.

### Common Timing Mistakes

- Short players waiting for the smoke to fully bloom before moving. They arrive after CTs have already pushed.
- Flash thrown before players are in position to swing. CTs turn and the flash is wasted.
- Second smoke thrown before the team has decided the site. It lands on the wrong side of the map.
- Lurk peeking before the main utility and contact have happened. The lurk dies early and the team loses the late timing.

## How the Top IGLs Play It in 2026

The six IGLs split into two camps. Some build the pistol around one star's duel (magixx, karrigan, Twistzz). Others build it around a structure (Aleksib, apEX, FalleN). apEX sits in the middle, structure with ropz's lurk as the flex, and Vitality have the best pistol numbers in the world.

A note on evidence: nobody publishes clean round-by-round T-pistol data per team per map. Each section below pairs sourced 2026 facts (rosters, results, role stats) with a read of how that IGL builds the round. Where a claim is a read rather than a stat, it is marked as one.

### Vitality: apEX and the Best Pistol Team in the World

Vitality are the benchmark: an estimated 60.5% pistol win rate from Sep 2025 to Jun 2026, third of 101 tracked teams and first among elite sides ([Esports Oracle](https://esportsoracle.net/cs2/stats/pistol-round); the estimate credits a pistol to whoever won the half). The roster is unchanged: apEX (IGL), ZywOo, ropz, flameZ, mezii ([game-tournaments](https://game-tournaments.com/en/csgo/team/vitality)).

**2026 so far.** Titles at IEM Kraków, PGL Cluj-Napoca, BLAST Open Rotterdam, IEM Rio, BLAST Rivals Season 1, StarSeries Fall and ESL Pro League Season 24 ([Wikipedia: ropz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ropz)). The big miss was the Cologne Major quarter-final, lost to Falcons on a Dust2 decider ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/matches/2394999/falcons-vs-vitality-iem-cologne-major-2026)).

**Dust2 is not automatic for them.** They crushed Spirit 13–5 on Spirit's own Dust2 pick in the IEM Rio final ([dust2.us](https://www.dust2.us/matches/2393245/spirit-vs-vitality)) and FURIA 13–4 at BLAST Open Porto. They also lost their own Dust2 pick to NAVI in Atlanta and the Cologne decider to Falcons.

**The roles.** Under apEX, Vitality play around information from flameZ in aggressive areas and ropz lurking on the other side of the map ([winio](https://winio.ai/blog/articles/top-10-teams-cs2-in-2026)).

**What to expect (our read):**

- apEX takes the dirty first entry with a flash so ZywOo can trade with a Glock.
- ropz's lurk is the weapon. One player holding lower tunnels or mid on pistol punishes the CT who rotates early.
- flameZ supplies the early contact that tells apEX where the third CT is. The call follows the information, not a fixed plan.
- Fewer set plays than NAVI, more mid-round calls. That flexibility is why their pistol rate holds across every map.

### Spirit: magixx Builds the Round Around donk

Spirit's T pistol is the most player-centred in the elite: everything exists to give donk a flashed duel at the front of the hit. magixx took over the calling for 2026 with zont1x back in the side ([cs2.ad](https://blog.cs2.ad/spirit-cs2/)). The Major lineup was magixx, tN1R, sh1ro, donk and zont1x ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/matches/2395001/spirit-vs-falcons-iem-cologne-major-2026)).

**The system change.** Spirit used to throw fewer flashes than other teams. In 2026 sh1ro's and tN1R's flash output went up, and donk's opening-kill success jumped from 58.2% in 2025 to 72.9% early in 2026 ([esports.net](https://www.esports.net/news/counter-strike/why-changes-to-spirits-system-will-make-donk-unstoppable-in-2026/)). magixx takes about 18% of T-side opening duels at 37% success, and his deaths get traded more often than chopper's did.

**On Dust2.** One of their best maps, with a 71.2% win rate in CSDB's tracked sample ([CSDB](https://csdb.gg/maps/dust2/)). In 2026 they beat Falcons 13–7 at IEM Rio and 16–12 in the PGL Astana final, lost 5–13 to Vitality in the Rio final, and lost 12–16 in overtime to Falcons in the Cologne semi-final. At the Esports World Cup they won four Dust2 maps, including 19–15 in overtime against Luminosity ([fragcharts](https://fragcharts.com/maps/dust2)).

**What to expect (our read):**

- donk on point, every time. Two teammates flash for him out of upper tunnels or over short.
- sh1ro plays pistol as support: utility carrier and the trade behind donk, not a second entry.
- magixx floats mid for the early opening or the info that tells donk which site is thin.
- The counter: stack the site donk is going to and survive the first flash. When Spirit lose pistols, it is usually because donk's duel came unflashed.

### Falcons: karrigan's Major-Winning Gamble

Falcons won the Cologne Major with a Dust2 T side that was never their strength. They got there on CT halves, overtime nerve and karrigan giving his stars freedom. karrigan left FaZe in April 2026 and reunited with NiKo and coach zonic ([Refrag](https://refrag.gg/blog/cs2-news-roundup-april-2026)). The lineup is karrigan (IGL), NiKo, m0NESY, TeSeS and kyousuke.

**2026 so far.** A rough debut, losing the PGL Astana final 3–0 to Spirit with karrigan on a 0.66 rating ([esportnow](https://esportnow.gg/cs2/news/spirit-pgl-astana-2026-champions-falcons-donk-mvp)), then the Major title over Vitality, Spirit and FURIA ([Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/team-falcons-become-iem-cologne-major-2026-champions)).

**Their Dust2 story is all deciders.**

- Lost Dust2 to Vitality 6–13 and Spirit 7–13 at IEM Rio, and 12–16 in overtime to Spirit in the Astana final.
- Then won two Major playoff deciders on it: Vitality in the quarter-final, Spirit in the semi-final.
- The semi-final tells the whole story. Falcons started T and won 5 of 12, took the CT half 7–5, then won overtime 4–0 for 16–12 ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/matches/2395001/spirit-vs-falcons-iem-cologne-major-2026)). Spirit finally won a pistol on that map after dropping them earlier in the series ([dust2.us](https://www.dust2.us/news/75216/furia-and-falcons-book-their-spot-in-the-iem-cologne-major-final)).

**What to expect (our read):**

- Simple, fast pistols: one site, stacked flashes, karrigan as the first body through the door. His 0.74 series rating in that semi-final fits an IGL who dies first on purpose.
- NiKo is the Glock threat at the front of short A or out of upper tunnels.
- m0NESY plays pistols for picks, often mid, not as an entry.
- The weakness is mid control. A Dust2 T pistol that skips mid lets one CT rotator through B doors collapse the hit, which is what Spirit exploited at Rio and Astana.

### NAVI: Aleksib's Structured Overload

NAVI's T pistol is a rehearsed, utility-first hit where Aleksib spends himself so makazze and b1t can trade into the site. The lineup is Aleksib, iM, b1t, w0nderful and makazze ([Leetify](https://leetify.com/pro-hub/team/natus-vincere)).

**2026 so far.** NAVI won ESL Pro League Season 23 and IEM Atlanta ([Strafe](https://www.strafe.com/news/read/navi-dominates-gamer-legion-to-win-iem-atlanta-2026/)), beating Vitality 13–11 on Vitality's own Dust2 pick on the way ([dust2.us](https://www.dust2.us/matches/2394174/natus-vincere-vs-vitality)). Cologne went badly: 0–1 on Dust2 at the Major, and the teamplay that gave them a high floor went missing while makazze struggled individually ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/iem-cologne-2026-major-biggest-winners-and-losers/)).

**The roles.** At Cologne, makazze led NAVI in opening duels, Aleksib in traded deaths and b1t in trade kills ([Leetify](https://leetify.com/pro-hub/team/natus-vincere)). That is the shape of the round: makazze opens, Aleksib takes the second contact and dies traded, b1t cleans up.

**What to expect (our read):**

- A prepared hit, not a default. More utility than kevlar, one site committed early.
- B through upper tunnels fits best: Aleksib or iM smokes CT or window, the doors flash goes for makazze, b1t is the trade.
- w0nderful plays as a Glock rifler on the mid or long kevlar, holding the rotator off rather than entering.
- When it fails, it fails on the trade, not the entry. If b1t is late, Aleksib's sacrifice buys nothing.

Aleksib said before Cologne Stage 3 that strategies shown in earlier stages are hard to hide later ([Players](https://players.com.ua/en/news/aleksib-i-consider-fut-and-legacy-dark-horses-historically-they-ve-beaten-us-twice-in-a-row/)). A one-off pistol set piece is the cleanest place to keep something new in reserve.

### FURIA: FalleN's Hybrid, Still Solving Dust2

FURIA reached the Cologne Major final, but Dust2 is where their T side has looked least finished in 2026. The lineup is FalleN (IGL), yuurih, YEKINDAR, KSCERATO and molodoy ([game-tournaments](https://game-tournaments.com/en/csgo/team/furia)), built in 2025 when FalleN handed the AWP to molodoy and moved to rifle to focus on calling ([sih.app](https://blog.sih.app/?p=5061)).

**2026 so far.** Runner-up at IEM Kraków and the Cologne Major, fourth at IEM Rio. The Major final was a 3–0 loss to Falcons.

**On Dust2.** Vitality beat them 13–4 at BLAST Open Porto and again on Dust2 at StarSeries Fall ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/matches/2398102/vitality-vs-furia-starladder-starseries-fall-2026)), and Falcons took the PGL Astana decider on it ([HLTV](https://www.hltv.org/matches/2394152/furia-vs-falcons-pgl-astana-2026)). At the Esports World Cup they beat 9z 13–6 and G2 13–4, then lost 13–16 to FUT and 9–13 to Legacy ([fragcharts](https://fragcharts.com/maps/dust2)).

**What to expect (our read):**

- YEKINDAR is the entry, bringing the aggressive first contact FURIA's naturally passive riflers lacked.
- FalleN calls a structured, mid-first pistol: take Xbox and mid doors, then choose the site off the rotation.
- KSCERATO and yuurih are the trade and the post-plant anchor, the most reliable part of the round.
- molodoy usually takes the long or mid kevlar for a pick, not a spot in the stack.
- Vitality have read and punished the mid-first plan. A faster B pistol is the obvious adjustment.

FaZe's 2026 T pistol is a rebuild in progress: a new caller, a new AWPer, and firepower doing more of the work than set pieces. karrigan's April exit was sudden by Twistzz's own account ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/twistzz-all-but-confirms-karrigans-faze-departure-can-falcons-move-work/)). **The current five.** Twistzz (IGL), frozen, JBOEN, jcobbb and Neityu, coached by enkay J. broky was benched over the summer and BIG Academy AWPer JBOEN signed ([Refrag](https://refrag.gg/blog/cs2-2026-rostermania-summer)). Neityu went from stand-in to a permanent deal as a low-resource, selfless piece ([Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/faze-sign-neityu-permanent-deal/)).

**On Dust2.** Their marquee Dust2 of the second half was an 11–13 loss to Vitality at the Esports World Cup ([fragcharts](https://fragcharts.com/maps/dust2)). Close against the best team in the world, which sums up where FaZe sit.

**What to expect (our read):**

- Neityu is the utility carrier and first body on a B hit, freeing frozen and jcobbb for the trades.

- JBOEN is a very aggressive young AWPer. On pistol that points to an early mid or long duel with kevlar.

### The 2026 Trends

- **Flash support for the star is the new standard.** donk's numbers moved because teammates started flashing for him, not because he aimed better.
- **Dust2 is a decider map.** The two biggest Dust2 maps of the year, both Cologne playoff deciders, went to the team that won the CT half and overtime, not the better T pistol.

- **Post-plants changed in July.** Walls now block bomb damage, so pistol post-plants no longer guarantee a kill on the saving CT.

## Stealing It for Your Own Team

1. **Name your donk.** Decide who wins the duel and make two teammates flash for him. This is Spirit's whole 2026 upgrade, and it works at every level.
2. **Name your Aleksib.** Someone dies first on purpose. Agree who, and agree who trades. The trade is what NAVI lost at Cologne.
3. **Keep one lurker alive.** A ropz-style player in lower tunnels or mid catches the early rotator and turns a 4v5 into a won round.
4. **Have a B and an A version.** Fast B through upper tunnels against a 2–1–2; mid-to-B or short A against anyone who stacks B.
5. **Buy utility, not five kevlars.** A smoke and two flashes for the entry win more pistols than armor on the riflers.
6. **Plan round 2 before round 1.** Know your anti-eco positions: hold long and B doors, and do not chase into SMG range.

## Practice Order

1. Run only the Xbox smoke into short default for 10–15 rounds. No executes until the opening is clean and no one dies in the first ten seconds.
2. Add the A hit once short control is automatic.
3. Add the B call and the long lurk last.
4. Only add fakes or distraction peeks after the core system is consistent.

### Practice Sequence for Utility

1. One player throws Xbox smoke on freeze. Two players move to short on the throw call. Time it so they reach short as the smoke blooms.
2. Add one flash thrown by the second short player as the contact player reaches the edge.
3. Once that is clean, add the long hold and the decision call inside the smoke window.
4. Only then add a second smoke or molotov.

Call sequence: "Xbox smoke, two short." Contact player takes the fight inside the smoke window. IGL calls the branch before the smoke fades: "A hit," "B," or "rotate." No re-peek if the first two die without a trade.

Pistols are won by having a second plan when the first contact dies, not by out-aiming the other team in the open. Copy the default, assign the roles the way the players above actually play them, and stop freelancing after the first death. That is the full system.
