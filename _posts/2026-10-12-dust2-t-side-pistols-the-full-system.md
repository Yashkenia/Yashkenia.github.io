Pistol rounds on Dust2 are decided by the opening script and the first decision after contact. The teams that win them start with mid control, assign one clear contact role, and only branch once they have information. Here is the full system with the additional execution details, timings, and variations used by Spirit, Vitality, Falcons, and NaVi.

## Buys and Roles

Most players run Glock. One player buys a P250 and armor. That player is the contact player and takes the first mid-range fight. One kit is optional on the lurk or plant player. Utility is one Xbox or mid smoke and one flash. Armor is non-negotiable. The P250 goes to the player most likely to win the first duel, not the player who asks for it.

**Contact player (donk style)**  
P250 + armor. Plays short or mid. Creates the opening. If this player gets the kill, flood. Does not lurk.

**Second short player (NiKo style)**  
Trades the first contact or takes the second angle. Can throw the supporting flash or smoke. Supports the entry rather than pure lurking.

**Support / late angle (m0NESY style)**  
Avoids the first dry fight. Holds a longer angle once utility is down or throws a flash after space is taken. Secondary contact or support on pistol.

**IGL / mid connector (Karrigan or Aleksib style)**  
Calls the Xbox smoke. Coordinates utility. Reads the first 10–15 seconds and decides the branch. Stays with mid or short rather than forcing early contact. Prefers space before full commitment. Can play the mid-to-lower tunnels link.

**Fifth player**  
Pure lurk or tunnels watcher. Stays quiet until the main fight creates a sound.

## Default Opening (0–15 seconds)

One player throws the Xbox smoke immediately. Two players move to short as soon as it lands. One of them is the P250 contact player. One player holds outside long and does not peek. One player waits mid or lurks long. The fifth stays available.

The smoke denies the CT mid player a free kill. Short players do not need kills. They need to exist so the team can choose. This is the shared starting point across the teams above.

Spirit’s Budapest Major version against Falcons added a short molotov after the Xbox smoke. Three players climbed cat. A flash was thrown once the molotov ended. Chopper smoked CT. Short players swung through the defensive smoke. Donk took two with the P250. Two players timed long. They took A and won a 5v3 post-plant. Vitality later countered the same structure by placing a player under the drop who killed the first player coming down.

Vitality’s variation leans harder on mid presence. The mid duo creates contact or forces utility. Lurkers on long and short stay quiet until CTs commit, then take the empty side or the late plant. Newer stacks can copy the principle: two mid (one with P250), one lurk long, one short, one waiting. The mid players only need to create a sound or a death that tells the team which site is lighter.

## Decision Rules (15–25 seconds)

After the smoke lands and short is taken:

- Short free or only light contact → call A hit.
- Heavy long early or multiple CTs long → call B rush.
- P250 player gets the first kill → flood that site.
- First two players die without a trade → remaining three do not re-peek the same angle. Rotate or save.

These are the only branches. Everything else is freestyle and loses rounds.

## A Hit Execute

Two players long, two players short or cat, one lurk. Throw a long or CT smoke if available. Long players take the first contact. Cat players wait for the sound and swing. Do not both peek dry at the same time. Plant default or for long. Post-plant holds: one long, one site, one CT or mid. Do not chase. The lurk only moves once the main fight has started.

Timing note: utility should land with roughly 1:20–1:30 remaining so the team has time to clear and plant without being forced into a late retake.

## B Rush

All five players tunnels. One flash if available. Do not stop in tunnels. Plant default. Hold window and site. If the first two die without a trade, the rest save or full rotate. Speed is the only advantage. FURIA and Spirit have both used versions of this when they want a fast plant and a simple post-plant.

## Additional Variations

**Mid fake into B**  
Two players make noise or throw utility mid or short. Three players are already in tunnels. The fake lasts 8–12 seconds. If CTs rotate, commit B. If they do not, continue the A hit instead of forcing B.

**Long timing lurk**  
One player goes long alone and waits. The other four play mid or short. The lurk does not peek first. He moves only after hearing the main fight. NaVi and Spirit have used this to punish early over-rotates. For newer players the rule is simple: if you are the lurk, you wait for the sound.

**Distraction peek**  
One player wide peeks or bunny hops long or mid with no intention of winning the duel. The only job is to force a CT to shoot and reveal. The other four are already moving to the opposite site. Only run this if the team knows the plan. Otherwise the distraction player dies for free.

## Post-Plant Defaults

- **A site**: one long, one site, one CT or mid. Do not stack site.
- **B site**: one window, one site, one mid or CT. Window is the priority hold.
- One player always watches the most likely CT entry (CT for A, mid for B).

## Practice Order

1. Run only the Xbox smoke into short default for 10–15 rounds. No executes until the opening is clean and no one dies in the first ten seconds.
2. Add the A hit once short control is automatic.
3. Add the B call and the long lurk last.
4. Only add fakes or distraction peeks after the core system is consistent.

Call sequence: “Xbox smoke, two short.” Contact player takes the fight. IGL reads the result. Then “A hit,” “B,” or “rotate.” No re-peek if the first two die without a trade.

Pistols are won by having a second plan when the first contact dies, not by out-aiming the other team in the open. Copy the default, assign the roles the way the players above actually play them, and stop freelancing after the first death. That is the full system.

## Utility Timing Details

Utility on pistol is limited (usually one smoke and one flash). Timing is what makes it effective. The goal is to land the smoke before players expose, use the flash to cover the swing, and avoid throwing utility that lands after the fight has already started. Here are the timings that match the structures used by Spirit, Vitality, and similar teams.

### Xbox / Mid Smoke Timing

Throw the Xbox smoke immediately after freeze time. It should land and bloom before the short players reach the corner of short. This takes roughly 2–3 seconds from throw to full cover. Short players start moving as soon as the throw is called, not after the smoke has already landed. If they wait for the smoke to fully bloom before moving, they lose the timing window and give CTs time to push short or mid.

In Spirit’s Budapest Major version against Falcons, Chopper threw the Xbox smoke first, then jumped down and molotov’d short. The molotov was used to delay or clear short while the rest of the team climbed cat. The flash came after the molotov ended so players could swing through the defensive smoke CTs threw. The sequence was smoke → molotov → flash → swing. Newer teams can drop the molotov and keep only the smoke + flash if money is tight.

### Flash Timing

The flash is thrown once short or long players are in position to swing, not earlier. On short, the flash should pop as the contact player reaches the edge of short or just before the swing. A flash thrown too early lets CTs turn away. A flash thrown too late leaves players peeking dry.

On an A hit, the long flash is thrown as the long players reach the doors or just before they peek. The cat or short flash is thrown on the sound of the long contact so the second angle arrives while CTs are already looking the other way. Do not throw both flashes at the same time with no coordination. One flash covers the first contact. The second covers the trade swing.

### Long or CT Smoke Timing

If a second smoke is available, throw the long or CT smoke after the Xbox smoke has already given short control. The long smoke should land before the long players commit past the doors. The CT smoke is used once the team has decided to hit A and wants to block the rotate. Throwing the CT smoke too early wastes it if the team ends up going B. Throwing it too late leaves the rotate open during the plant.

### B Rush Utility

On a full B rush there is usually no smoke. If one flash is available, throw it as the first players reach the end of tunnels, just before they exit onto site. The flash should pop as the entry players clear the doors or window angle. Do not throw the flash from deep in tunnels. It will pop behind the entry players and do nothing.

### Decision Timing Relative to Utility

The Xbox smoke creates a 15–20 second window of information. The IGL should make the site call inside that window, not after the smoke has fully faded. If short is free while the smoke is still up, call the A hit or mid-to-B before the smoke dies. If heavy long contact happens while the smoke is still active, call B immediately. Waiting until the smoke fades to decide usually means the team is already late and CTs have rotated.

### Common Timing Mistakes

- Short players waiting for the smoke to fully bloom before moving. They arrive after CTs have already pushed.
- Flash thrown before players are in position to swing. CTs turn and the flash is wasted.
- Second smoke thrown before the team has decided the site. It lands on the wrong side of the map.
- Lurk peeking before the main utility and contact have happened. The lurk dies early and the team loses the late timing.

### Practice Sequence for Utility

1. One player throws Xbox smoke on freeze. Two players move to short on the throw call. Time it so they reach short as the smoke blooms.
2. Add one flash thrown by the second short player as the contact player reaches the edge.
3. Once that is clean, add the long hold and the decision call inside the smoke window.
4. Only then add a second smoke or molotov.

The call stays the same: “Xbox smoke, two short.” Contact player takes the fight inside the smoke window. IGL calls the branch before the smoke fades. No re-peek if the first two die without a trade. Utility only works if the movement and the decision happen on the same timing.
